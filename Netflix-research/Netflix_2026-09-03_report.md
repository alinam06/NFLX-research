# Lab 04: Netflix Company Research Report

## Executive summary

Netflix reported higher revenue, operating income, operating margin, net income, and operating cash flow in 2025 than in 2024. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 20-21 and 27.)*

The company remained primarily dependent on monthly membership fees, while increased advertising revenue contributed to its 2025 revenue growth. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 20-21.)*

Netflix also carried significant debt and content obligations, continued substantial share repurchases, and disclosed risks involving member retention, competition, content commitments, advertising, international operations, regulation, privacy, and technology. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 4-15; Item 7, pp. 24 and 26; Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

## Company and filing overview

Netflix, Inc. is an entertainment service offering television series, films, games, and live programming across many genres and languages. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 3.)*

The company primarily earns revenue from monthly membership fees for streaming services and also earns revenue from advertising, consumer products, live experiences, and other sources. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 3; Item 7, p. 20.)*

Netflix's common stock trades on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker **NFLX**. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, cover page, p. 1; Item 5, p. 18.)*

This report analyzes Netflix's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed January 23, 2026 under SEC accession number 0001065280-26-000034. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, cover page, p. 1; SEC EDGAR filing-detail page, January 23, 2026.)*

## Business model and operating priorities

Netflix's core strategy is to grow globally while improving the member experience by offering compelling content, a range of pricing plans, and an expanding advertising business. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 3-4.)*

Monthly membership fees remained the company's primary revenue source in 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

Revenue from advertising, consumer products, live experiences, and other non-membership-fee sources was not material in 2025, 2024, or 2023. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

Netflix discontinued reporting membership counts during 2025 and instead identified revenue and operating margin as the primary financial metrics that management believes best represent business performance. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

For this report, revenue is therefore retained as the principal operating-value driver from the original analysis. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20; author judgment.)*

## 2025 financial performance

Netflix generated 2025 revenue of **$45.183 billion**, compared with **$39.001 billion** in 2024, an increase of **16%**. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 20-21.)*

Management attributed the increase primarily to membership growth, price increases, and higher advertising revenue, partly offset by unfavorable foreign-exchange movements net of hedging. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 21.)*

Operating income rose to **$13.327 billion** from **$10.418 billion**, a **28%** increase. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

Operating margin increased to **29.5%** from **26.7%**, mainly because revenue grew faster than cost of revenues, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

Net income increased to **$10.981 billion** from **$8.712 billion**, a **26%** increase. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

Management said the increase in net income primarily reflected higher operating income, partly offset by a larger provision for income taxes. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 20.)*

## Revenue profile

2025 streaming revenue was **$19.957 billion** in the United States and Canada, **$14.515 billion** in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, **$5.358 billion** in Latin America, and **$5.354 billion** in Asia-Pacific. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 21.)*

Reported streaming-revenue growth was **15%** in the United States and Canada, **17%** in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, **11%** in Latin America, and **21%** in Asia-Pacific. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 21.)*

Netflix stated that foreign-exchange movements reduced reported growth relative to constant-currency growth in some regions. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 21.)*

## Cost structure and content economics

Cost of revenues was **$23.275 billion** in 2025, compared with **$21.038 billion** in 2024. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 39.)*

Content amortization was **$16.422 billion** in 2025, including **$8.714 billion** for licensed content and **$7.709 billion** for produced content. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 5, p. 51.)*

Netflix generally expects more than 90% of a licensed or produced content asset to be amortized within four years after its first month of availability. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 1, p. 45.)*

Content accounting depends on management estimates of future viewing patterns, and the independent auditor identified content amortization as a critical audit matter because those estimates are subjective. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Report of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm, pp. 37-38.)*

## Cash flow, liquidity, and debt

Netflix generated **$10.149 billion** of net cash from operating activities in 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

The operating-cash-flow statement included **$17.097 billion** of additions to content assets and **$16.422 billion** of content-asset amortization. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

Netflix reported **$9.034 billion** of cash and cash equivalents and **$28.678 million** of short-term investments at December 31, 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 42.)*

Cash and cash equivalents exclude short-term investments and restricted cash. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 42; Note 4, p. 49.)*

Short-term and long-term debt had a combined carrying amount of **$14.463 billion**, net of issuance costs and discounts, down **$1.120 billion** from 2024. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Liquidity and Capital Resources, p. 24.)*

The balance-sheet components were **$998.865 million** of short-term debt and **$13.464 billion** of long-term debt. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 42.)*

Note 7 separately reported approximately **$14.519 billion** of aggregate debt principal, which differs from the carrying amount because the latter reflects issuance costs and discounts. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 7, p. 54.)*

Netflix repaid approximately **$1.833 billion** of debt in 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Liquidity and Capital Resources, p. 24; Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

Known contractual obligations totaled **$45.029 billion** at year-end, including **$24.039 billion** of content obligations, **$18.092 billion** of principal and interest on debt, and **$2.898 billion** of operating-lease obligations. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Material Cash Requirements, p. 26.)*

Of the content obligations, **$18.4 billion** had not yet been recognized on the balance sheet because the recognition criteria had not been met. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Material Cash Requirements, p. 26.)*

## Capital allocation

Netflix spent **$9.127 billion** in cash on common-stock repurchases in 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

The company had **$8.0 billion** remaining under its share-repurchase authorization at December 31, 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Share Repurchases, p. 26.)*

Netflix has never declared or paid a cash dividend and stated that it did not anticipate paying one in the foreseeable future. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 5, p. 18.)*

## Planned acquisition disclosed in the filing

Netflix reported an amended agreement to acquire Warner Bros. Discovery's streaming and studios businesses, including HBO Max and HBO, after the planned separation of Discovery Global. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Other Planned Uses of Cash and Debt Capital, p. 27.)*

The filing described an estimated **$72.0 billion equity value** and **$82.7 billion enterprise value**, both measured as of December 4, 2025, and stated that closing remained subject to regulatory approval, WBD shareholder approval, the separation of Discovery Global, and other conditions. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Other Planned Uses of Cash and Debt Capital, p. 27.)*

Those figures and conditions described the transaction as of the 10-K filing date. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, Other Planned Uses of Cash and Debt Capital, p. 27.)*

On February 26, 2026, Netflix announced that it would not raise its offer after WBD determined that Paramount Skydance's proposal was superior; Netflix said the higher matching price would no longer be financially attractive. *(Source: Netflix Investor Relations press release, February 26, 2026.)*

Netflix also said it expected to invest approximately **$20 billion** in films and series during 2026 and resume share repurchases. *(Source: Netflix Investor Relations press release, February 26, 2026.)*

## Share count and per-share data

Netflix reported **4.344 billion** weighted-average diluted shares for 2025 and diluted earnings per share of **$2.53**. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 39.)*

The diluted share count consisted of **4.250 billion** weighted-average basic shares plus **94.351 million** dilutive stock-based awards. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 3, p. 49.)*

All share and per-share amounts were retroactively adjusted for the ten-for-one forward stock split completed on November 14, 2025. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 1, p. 44; Note 3, p. 49.)*

## Principal risks disclosed by Netflix

Netflix identified the ability to attract and retain members as a central business risk because growth requires replacing canceled memberships and adding new members. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, p. 4.)*

The company competes for consumers' leisure time against linear television, streaming entertainment, gaming, open-content platforms, social media, and other activities. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 4-5.)*

Netflix disclosed that competition can affect its ability to obtain content, attract and retain members, and maintain or increase revenue. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 4-5.)*

Content commitments are long-term and largely fixed, which can limit the company's flexibility if revenue growth disappoints or particular content is not favorably received. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 10-11.)*

The advertising business exposes Netflix to risks involving advertiser demand, measurement, privacy rules, technology, fraud, and competition for advertising spending. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 6-8.)*

International operations expose the company to foreign-exchange movements, local regulation, political and economic instability, and varying cultural preferences. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 11-13.)*

Netflix also identified cybersecurity, privacy, service disruption, intellectual-property, personnel, and stock-price-volatility risks. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 8-15.)*

## Prior management commitments and verification

### Commitment made in the 2023 Form 10-K

Management said Netflix expected to continue significant investment in global content, particularly original content, and anticipated that operating cash flow, available funds, and financing access would cover cash needs for at least the next twelve months. *(Source: Netflix 2023 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 26; filed January 26, 2024.)*

**Outcome:** The content-investment commitment was fulfilled directionally: content-asset additions increased from **$12.555 billion** in 2023 to **$16.224 billion** in 2024, while content amortization increased from **$14.197 billion** to **$15.302 billion**. *(Source: Netflix 2024 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 39.)*

**Outcome:** The liquidity expectation was also met over the following year: Netflix generated **$7.361 billion** of operating cash flow in 2024 and ended 2024 with **$9.586 billion** of cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments. *(Source: Netflix 2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 23-25.)*

### Commitment made in the 2024 Form 10-K

Management again said Netflix expected significant global and original-content investment and anticipated sufficient liquidity for the next twelve months and beyond. *(Source: Netflix 2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 24; filed January 27, 2025.)*

**Outcome:** The content-investment commitment was fulfilled directionally: content-asset additions rose to **$17.097 billion** in 2025 from **$16.224 billion** in 2024, and content amortization rose to **$16.422 billion** from **$15.302 billion**. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

**Outcome:** The liquidity expectation was met over the following year: Netflix generated **$10.149 billion** of operating cash flow in 2025, repaid **$1.833 billion** of debt, and ended the year with **$9.068 billion** of cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 24 and 27; Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

### Assessment of the record

Both repeated commitments were followed by results consistent with management's stated direction: content investment continued and liquidity remained sufficient through each following year. *(Source: Netflix 2023 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 26; Netflix 2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 24; Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 24 and 27.)*

This comparison tests only the specific commitments quoted above; it does not imply that every forecast or strategic objective in the prior filings was achieved. *(Source: research methodology for Lab 04, September 3, 2026.)*

## Research assessment

The filing shows strong 2025 revenue growth, expanding operating margin, higher net income, and substantial operating cash flow. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 20-21; Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

The filing also shows material content spending, significant debt, large share repurchases, and business risks tied to member retention, competition, advertising execution, content commitments, foreign exchange, regulation, and technology. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 4-15; Item 7, p. 24; Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 41.)*

Taken together, the reported results show stronger 2025 operating performance, while Netflix's content commitments, debt, competitive environment, and execution demands remain important considerations when assessing the company. *(Source: Netflix 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 4-15; Item 7, pp. 20-21, 24, and 26.)*
